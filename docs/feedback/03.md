Priority: **P1**. Area: Ancillary aggregation / client mappings.

## Evidence and scope

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two isolates with one shared profile and different countries produce a 50/50 country pie.
- [ ] Three isolates with two countries produce 2/3 and 1/3; missing metadata is explicit and never changes isolate totals silently.
- [ ] Define multi-field combinations and denominators, consistently across nodes, legend and ancillary wheel.
- [ ] Counts and colors remain consistent across expansion, filtering and viewport changes.
